Free launch fees ≠ free transactions

Zero platform launch fees means Launchova does not charge a mandatory fee to create and deploy a token through the platform. This does not cover:

  • Blockchain network gas costs, which are paid to the network, not to Launchova.
  • Any applicable trading fees set by the project or mechanism.

Launchova does not promise free transactions unless gas sponsorship is actually implemented.

Core Feature · Planned

Different Launches. One Ecosystem.

Launchova is designed to support different launch approaches so creators can choose the mechanism that fits their project.

Two intended launch options are described below. Specific bonding curve parameters, graduation thresholds, and liquidity guarantees have not been defined and will be published before launch.
Option A · Planned

Bonding Curve Launches

Tokens begin with a predefined bonding curve. As tokens are bought and sold, pricing changes according to the curve's rules, and users can follow launch progress in real time.

Eligible tokens may graduate into a liquidity pool after reaching their defined graduation conditions.

  • ●Predefined bonding curve sets initial pricing
  • ●Price adjusts per the curve's rules
  • ●Launch progress is trackable
  • ●Eligible tokens can graduate to a liquidity pool
Option B · Planned

Direct Liquidity Launches

Where supported, creators may choose a direct liquidity launch instead of a bonding curve. Initial liquidity requirements and applicable liquidity information will be clearly displayed before launch.

Liquidity management and lock details will be disclosed so participants understand how liquidity is handled.

  • ●Direct liquidity launch where supported
  • ●Initial liquidity requirements disclosed
  • ●Liquidity information displayed upfront
  • ●Liquidity management and lock information shown

No bonding curve parameters, graduation thresholds, or liquidity guarantees are promised at this stage. All figures will be defined and published prior to product launch.
